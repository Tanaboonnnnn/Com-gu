This baseline was captured after the abandoned zvec Smart Search work was fully reverted and after the extension reload/synchronization fix was retained. It is the comparison point for the performance/footprint optimization plan; exact `find`/`rg` search behavior is the shipped search baseline.

## Packaged startup / idle RSS

Measured with the opt-in `COMGU_PERF_MARKER_FILE` ready marker in the packaged app. Normal launches do not write or read the marker path.

The first two 10-launch runs produced startup medians of 974 ms and 1,165 ms, which exceeded the plan's 10% repeatability gate. A third startup-only run immediately after the noisy sample produced a 958 ms median with a tight 879-1,061 ms range, within 1.7% of the first run. The second startup series is therefore retained as evidence of environmental jitter rather than used as the baseline center.

Accepted startup baseline (third run):

| Metric | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Cold-start samples | 10 |
| Minimum | 879 ms |
| Median | **958 ms** |
| Maximum | 1,061 ms |

Idle working-set/RSS was stable across the two five-sample 30-second series. The combined ten samples were:

Combined median idle RSS: **138,213,376 bytes (131.81 MiB)**.
